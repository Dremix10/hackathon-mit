## Rules

1. Create an account on app.hack-nation.ai if you have not already.
2. Choose one challenge and say which one in Discord.
3. Submit on **both** app.hack-nation.ai and the Google form. The form is the backup.
4. Every teammate needs an account and must be added under “team & submission”.

Submission checklist (upload to the app and the Google form):

| Item | Format | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo video | Video | Show the project |
| Tech video | Video | Explain the build |
| Team video | Video | Introduce the team |
| GitHub repo | **Public** link | Code and docs |
| Live demo | Vercel, Replit, or Lovable | Show the product |

Judges score technical depth, communication (docs, presentation, video), and innovation.

## This repo’s challenge

**Challenge 3 — Agentic Scientific Discovery** (Databricks). Build an AI lab that researches, forms hypotheses, plans and runs experiments, learns from results, and chooses the next experiment. Full brief: [challenge-brief.md](challenge-brief.md).

The other challenges announced at kickoff:

1. **The AI Apprentice** — voice apprentice that captures expert decisions and teaches the next person.
2. **Rental Housing Law Navigator** — address-level housing-law answers with citations, including pending law changes.
3. **Agentic Scientific Discovery** — this project.
4. **Small AI for Development** — offline-capable AI for health, agriculture, or tourism. Ages 18–35, valid passport. Winners travel to Seoul Oct 18–21 (Ignite Talks Oct 21). One representative per winning team, one team per sector.
5. **AI Atlas for Rare Diseases** (with OpenAI) — evidence-backed knowledge graph of diseases, genes, symptoms, studies, and patient groups.
